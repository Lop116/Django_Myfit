More information about visiting, memberships and current locations.

Can I train at different MyFit branches?

Access rules may depend on the membership plan you choose. The team can confirm cross-branch access before you join.

Where can I check opening hours?

Open a branch card to view its administrator-managed opening hours, if supplied.

How do I know if a branch is open?

Check the status displayed on each branch card. Coming-soon branches are not yet open.
