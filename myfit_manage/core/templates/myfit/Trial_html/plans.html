03 / GOOD TO KNOW

MEMBERSHIP QUESTIONS.

Questions about the monthly membership rates and the included benefits.

CONTACT MYFIT

What does each MyFit membership cost per month?

My Access is Rs 1,249/month, My Access XL is Rs 1,399/month and My Access Premium is Rs 1,549/month.

Does My Access XL include My Access benefits?

Yes. My Access XL includes the My Access benefits, as well as access to the MYGYM360 network (Réunion, Mauritius, France), membership suspension and unlimited group classes.

Which plans include unlimited group classes?

My Access XL includes unlimited group classes, and My Access Premium includes My Access XL benefits.

Which membership includes My Drink and My Sismo?

My Access Premium includes Unlimited My Drink and Unlimited My Sismo.
