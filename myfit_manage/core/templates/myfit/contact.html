03 / BEFORE YOU ASK

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A few helpful answers about MyFit's planned membership and digital services.

FIND YOUR
WAY FORWARD
How do I become a MyFit member?

Explore the options on our Membership Plans page, then use the registration link to begin creating an account. Online registration is part of the planned Django integration.

Where are the MyFit gym branches?

The project includes Caudan Waterfront in Port Louis, Emerald Park in Trianon and Riche Terre, with Plaisance Mall in Rose Belle marked as coming soon. Browse the Branches page for more information.

Can I explore classes before becoming a member?

Yes. Visit Classes to explore sample class programmes such as Strength, HIIT, Yoga and Zumba. Actual booking will require account functionality once the backend is connected.

What is the digital membership pass?

The planned digital pass displays your membership information and a QR/barcode for gym access and automated attendance tracking. Validation and check-in confirmation will be handled by the backend later.

How do support tickets work?

A member will submit a question or issue, then follow its status (open, in progress or resolved). The request is saved in MyFit and linked to your account when you are logged in.

How can I contact a coach?

Visit Our Coaches for the public coach directory, or select "Coaching" from the enquiry form above.
